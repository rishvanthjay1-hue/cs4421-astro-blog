# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on building systems capable of performing tasks that usually require human intelligence. These tasks include understanding language, recognising images, solving problems, and making predictions.

## How Does AI Work?

AI systems process information using algorithms. Some systems follow rules written by developers, while machine learning systems learn patterns from examples in data. Their results depend on the quality of their data, training, and design.

## AI in Everyday Life

AI is used in voice assistants, recommendation systems, navigation applications, spam filters, and customer support chatbots. These tools can help people complete tasks more quickly, although their results are not always correct.

## Benefits and Limitations

AI can automate repetitive work, analyse large amounts of information, and support decision-making. However, it can also make mistakes, reflect biases in its training data, and raise privacy concerns.

## Conclusion

Artificial intelligence is an important area of modern computing. Understanding its capabilities and limitations helps people use AI responsibly.
